Reference · organised the way Microsoft trains it

By app

The course is organised by task. This page is organised by app, so you can find the thing you are looking at after the day. Each section has what the app can see, two prompts, and one caution.

Microsoft’s own MS-4018 path follows the same shape. Links to the matching module are at the end of each section.

Outlook

What it sees. The selected email. With an add-on licence, your mailbox, calendar and related files.

Try this

Summarise this thread. List the decisions made, the actions with owner and date,
and the one thing I need to reply to. Mark anything unclear.
Reply to this email. Acknowledge the ask, answer the one question I can answer,
and ask for the two things I still need. Under 150 words, warm but plain.

Caution. Check the recipients, the attachments and every commitment before you send. Copilot drafts, you send.

Word

What it sees. The open document, and with a licence, files you point it to.

Try this

ROLE: You are an experienced proposal writer for a university executive education team.
TASK: Turn this brief into a proposal outline: context, client outcomes, modules,
delivery, fees, why Curtin.
CONTEXT: The reader is the client's finance lead. Use only what is in the brief.
Mark anything you inferred with [inferred]. BRIEF: [paste]
FORMAT: Six headings, one line each, plus questions to ask before writing.
Cut anything that would be true of any provider. Keep only what is specific to
this brief. Then list the claims that need evidence.

Caution. It will invent credentials, modules and claims. Read for what is not in the source.

Excel

What it sees. The open workbook, and the range or table you select.

Try this

What is wrong with this formula, and how do I fix it?
Explain the fix in plain English, as if I have to maintain it next year.
FORMULA: [paste]
Propose a budget table: activity, assumption, quantity, rate, total.
Leave rate blank. Do not invent rates. Give the total formula in plain English.
SCOPE: [paste]

Caution. A plausible rate is worse than a blank cell. Trace every number.

Copilot Chat and Edge

What it sees. The web, the page you are on, and anything you paste or upload. Work data only with an add-on licence and a tenant that allows it.

Try this

Ask me up to five questions before you start, one at a time, then summarise
this page for a busy executive. What are the three things that matter and
what is the one thing to check?
PAGE: [paste]
List what you could not determine from this material, and what a reader would
still need to know. Do not guess.

Caution. Chat is not connected to your inbox. If you need the email, use the Outlook pane or paste it. Close sensitive tabs before you share your screen.

Teams, if you use it

What it sees. The open chat or meeting. Recap and notes depend on transcription being enabled.

Try this

From this meeting, give me the decisions, the actions with owner and date,
and the three questions still open. Do not invent owners.

Caution. A recap is only as good as the transcript. If transcription was off, there is nothing to summarise.

The rule that applies everywhere

Check what the app can see, carry the context yourself, and read the output before it moves on. The responsible use page has the checklist.
