Session 5 · 11:45 to 12:15

Make it yours

This is the part that decides whether the morning sticks. Pick one recurring task and leave with a plan for it.

Map the workflow

Write your task as five steps:

Step What to write
Input What starts it. An enquiry, a brief, a meeting, a date.
Now What you do today, in order.
Pain Where it slows down or goes wrong.
Draft Where Copilot could produce a first draft.
Check Where a person must check before it moves on.

Common candidates for this team:

  • New client enquiry to a first proposal draft.
  • Post-program follow-up: feedback, summary, next-cohort pitch.
  • Marketing calendar: program description to a six-week campaign outline.
  • Client and BD pipeline: meeting notes to a CRM-style update and follow-up email.
  • Tender response: evaluation criteria to existing evidence.

Build the reusable prompt

Turn the task into an RTCF template with slots you fill each time.

ROLE: [who should write this]
TASK: [verb and scope]
CONTEXT: [reader, situation, standard, source]
FORMAT: [length, structure, tone]
CHECK: [what a person must verify]
NEVER: [what it must not invent or include]

Save it somewhere you will find it. A note, a Word file, a Copilot saved prompt if your licence has them. The best place is the one you already open every day.

Curious about agents? See Agents: the three tiers, and why a custom agent is a saved brief rather than automation.

Put the checkpoints in

For your workflow, mark where a person must check before the work moves on:

  • before anything goes to a client
  • before any figure is quoted
  • before any commitment is made
  • before anything is filed as a record

This is the answer to “where does AI fit, and will it work every week”. It works when the checkpoints are named and the handoffs are deliberate.

Commit

Write down:

  • two or three tasks you will try in the next week
  • the prompt you will start with
  • the checkpoint you will not skip
  • one thing you will stop doing

Five questions to take with you

  1. What is the task or challenge I am exploring?
  2. What is my first RTCF prompt?
  3. What did I like or not like about the response?
  4. What follow-up prompt would improve it?
  5. How might this become part of my routine?

Follow-up

Four weeks after the session, bring one example: what you tried, what stuck, what did not. A short check-in is enough. Three questions for the check-in:

  • Which task did you try, and how long did it take compared with before?
  • Where did Copilot help, and where did it get in the way?
  • What is the one checkpoint that saved you?
