Copy, fill, delete what does not fit

Prompt library

The habit behind all of these: say who you are, say who the reader is, paste the source, state the format, name the constraints, ask what is missing.

The template

You are helping a Curtin Executive Education team member.
Reader: [client / director / internal team]
Task: [what you need]
Source: [paste the brief, email, notes or document]
Format: [headings / table / email / 150 words]
Constraints: [tone, what to leave out, what not to invent]
Then list what you could not determine from the source.

Proposals and bids

Brief to outline

Here is a client brief. Produce a proposal outline with these sections:
context, client outcomes, proposed modules, delivery approach, fees, why Curtin.
Use only what is in the brief. Mark anything you inferred with [inferred].
Then list the questions we should ask the client before writing.
BRIEF: [paste]

Sceptical client critique

Act as a sceptical client evaluating this proposal. List:
what makes you doubt the provider, which claims are unsupported,
where it answers a question you did not ask, and what is missing
against the stated criteria. Do not rewrite it. Give a revision plan.
PROPOSAL: [paste]

Tender criteria mapping

Here are the evaluation criteria and our evidence. Build a table with
criterion, weighting, our evidence, the gap, and what to write.
Do not invent evidence. Where we have none, say so.
CRITERIA: [paste]
EVIDENCE: [paste]

Marketing organisation

Program to campaign

Turn this program description into a six-week campaign outline for
[a professional audience]. Include channel, message, asset needed and owner.
Keep the tone [practical / warm / formal]. No more than one line per item.
PROGRAM: [paste]

Audience rewrite

Rewrite this for [a busy HR director / a first-time participant / an academic board].
Same facts. Shorter sentences. Lead with what they get. Do not add claims.
TEXT: [paste]

Client and business development

Meeting notes to follow-up

Turn these notes into: a five-line summary, decisions made, actions with
owner and date, and a short follow-up email to [name]. Do not invent
owners or dates. Flag anything unclear.
NOTES: [paste]

Enquiry to first response

A prospective client has asked about [program]. Draft a first response that
acknowledges the ask, asks the three questions we need answered, and offers
a short call. Warm, brief, no pricing. Enquiry: [paste]

Pipeline update

Turn this thread into a CRM-style update: client, stage, need, next step,
owner, date, risk. Keep it to one paragraph plus a table.
THREAD: [paste]

Excel

Structure a budget

Here is a scope list. Propose a budget table with columns for activity,
assumption, quantity, rate and total. Leave rate blank. Explain the formula
you would use for the total column.
SCOPE: [paste]

Explain a formula

Explain what this formula does in plain English, then rewrite it so a
non-technical colleague can maintain it. FORMULA: [paste]

Checking and critique

Gap check

List what you could not determine from this material, and what a reader
would still need to know. Do not guess.
MATERIAL: [paste]

Numbers check

Check every number in this document against the source. List each figure,
where it appears, and whether it matches.
DOCUMENT: [paste]
SOURCE: [paste]

Sycophancy test

Before you answer, list the two strongest reasons this plan might be wrong.
Then give your view. Do not agree with me by default.
PLAN: [paste]

Build your own

One template per recurring task, with slots you fill each time:

Task: [e.g. new enquiry to first proposal draft]
You are: [role]
Reader: [who]
Input: [what you will paste]
Steps: [the order you want]
Output: [format]
Check: [what must be verified by a person]
Never: [what it must not invent or include]
