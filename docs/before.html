Pre-work

Before the day

Three things, about ten minutes in total. The more of the survey you answer, the more the exercises use your work rather than a generic example.

1. Do the two-minute survey

The survey asks which tasks take longer than they should, and where you think AI could help in your role. It also asks whether you are willing to share a past brief or workflow.

Open the survey questions

Send your answers to Michael by Friday 30 October. If you would rather send a workflow or a past brief directly, do that instead. Sanitised is fine.

Do not include client names, participant names, pricing, margins or contract terms in your answers.

2. Check your Copilot label

Sign in at copilot.cloud.microsoft with your Curtin account. Look under your name. You will see one of these:

Label What it usually means
Copilot Chat (Basic) Web reasoning, plus whatever you open or upload. Limited work data.
M365 Copilot (Basic) Similar chat, with some app access depending on the tenant.
M365 Copilot (Premium) Adds work data through Microsoft Graph: mail, calendar, files, chats, meetings.

Write the label down. Bring it with you. The session looks different on different licences, and that difference is one of the things we will talk about.

Microsoft renamed some of these products in 2026. Microsoft 365 Copilot is now Microsoft Copilot, and Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat is now Microsoft Copilot Chat. The labels above are what you will see on screen. If yours says something else, note it anyway.

3. Bring one real task

Think of one task you do most weeks that takes longer than it should. Proposal drafting, follow-up emails, turning a brief into a program description, updating the pipeline, building a budget table. Any of them.

Bring it as a description, not a document. No client names.

What to bring on the day

  • A laptop with your Curtin account signed in.
  • Access to Outlook, Word and Excel.
  • Your Copilot label.
  • One task from your week.

What you do not need

  • Any technical background.
  • Any prompt-writing experience.
  • A perfect example. A rough task is enough.
