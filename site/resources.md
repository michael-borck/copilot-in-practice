# Resources

## Start with your own setup

- [Check your Copilot label](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft). Sign in with your Curtin account.
- [How Copilot Chat works with and without a licence](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/how-copilot-chat-works-with-and-without-a-microsoft-365-copilot-license). The clearest explanation of what the licence changes.
- [Chat with Copilot in Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/copilot-outlook/chat-with-copilot-in-outlook). What the Outlook pane can do.
- [Triage email with Copilot](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/triage-email-with-microsoft-365-copilot-in-outlook). Commands for flagging, moving and summarising.
- [Microsoft Copilot overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/microsoft-365-copilot-overview) and [release notes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/release-notes). Worth a skim once a month, because it changes.

## Companion masterclasses

These materials borrow the visual language and some of the framing from two Curtin Executive Education masterclasses by Michael Borck:

- [The Human Edge](https://michael-borck.github.io/the-human-edge/): using and delivering AI with judgement.
- [AI in Strategy](https://github.com/michael-borck/ai-in-strategy): choosing where to invest.
- [Conversation, Not Delegation](https://michael-borck.github.io/conversation-not-delegation/): the idea that AI work is a conversation you steer, not a task you hand over.

## Another Curtin course

[AI literacy for Teaching Support](https://github.com/michael-borck/curtin-tso-ai-literacy) is a two-hour Curtin course for Teaching Support Officers. It has a larger prompt playbook, more on prompt chaining, and a worked Excel and Word demo. The trade-offs table and the chaining pattern on this site are adapted from it.

## The repo

The source for this site is at [github.com/michael-borck/copilot-in-practice](https://github.com/michael-borck/copilot-in-practice). The course outline, prompt library, checklist, survey and sanitised briefs are all there. Reuse the structure if it helps your team.

## Follow-up

Four weeks after the session, bring one example: what you tried, what stuck, what did not. Send it to [michael.borck@curtin.edu.au](mailto:michael.borck@curtin.edu.au).
