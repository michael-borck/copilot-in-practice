# Pre-session survey

Two minutes. Send to the team by Monday 2 November. Ask for responses by Friday 30 October.

Purpose: build the session around the team's own work, not generic examples.

## Intro text for the email or form

> We are running a half-day Copilot session on Monday 9 November, 9:00 to 12:30 at Curtin Executive Education. To make it useful, I need two minutes from you. I want to know which tasks take longer than they should, and where you think AI could help in your role. I will use your answers to build the exercises. Please do not include client names or pricing in your answers. If you would rather send a workflow or a past brief directly, reply to this email.

## Questions

1. **Name and role**

2. **Which Copilot label do you see?** Sign in at `copilot.cloud.microsoft` and look under your name.
   - Copilot Chat (Basic)
   - M365 Copilot (Basic)
   - M365 Copilot (Premium)
   - Not sure / not set up

3. **Which tools do you use most days?** Tick all that apply.
   - Outlook
   - Word
   - Excel
   - PowerPoint
   - Teams
   - Copilot Chat
   - Edge

4. **List up to three tasks that take longer than they should.** One line each. Examples: drafting a proposal section, turning a brief into a program description, writing follow-up emails, updating the pipeline, building a budget table.

5. **For each task, roughly how long does it take now?** A rough number is fine.

6. **Where do you think AI could help in your role?** Two or three sentences.

7. **Where are you unsure or worried about using it?** For example client data, accuracy, sounding generic, not knowing where to start.

8. **Would you be willing to share one real example for the session?** A workflow, a past brief or a program description. Sanitised is fine.
   - Yes, I will send one
   - Yes, but use a sanitised version
   - No

9. **Anything you specifically want covered?** One line.

## Note on privacy

Do not include client names, participant names, pricing, margins or contract terms in your answers. If you want to describe a real example, describe the shape of it, not the client.
